Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Command Hub (operated by [your business name]) collects on this website and in the Command Hub app, and how it is used.

Information you give us

When you start a trial we collect your company name, your name, email address, phone number and a password (stored in scrambled form only). Roofers who use Command Hub store their customers' leads with us: names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and messages submitted through their website forms, calls and texts.

Information collected automatically

Our website records the pages you visit, buttons you click, and the campaign or email link that brought you here. It stores a random visitor ID in your browser to connect these visits. We use this to understand which of our messages are useful and to follow up with people who showed interest.

We may use advertising tags from Meta (Facebook) and Google to measure our ads and show ads to people who visited this site. These companies may use cookies or similar technology under their own privacy policies. You can limit this in your browser settings, at Facebook ad preferences, and at Google ad settings.

Text messages

If you submit a form on a roofer's website or call or text their Command Hub number, you may receive text messages from that roofer about your request. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to stop receiving texts, or HELP for help. We do not sell or share phone numbers or text-message consent with third parties for their marketing.

How we use information

To run the service (deliver leads and alerts, send texts and emails you set up), to support customers, to improve our website and marketing, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that help us run Command Hub, such as hosting (Render), text messaging (Twilio) and email delivery (Resend), and the advertising partners above. They may only use the information to provide their service to us. We may also share information if required by law.

Keeping and deleting information

We keep account and lead information while an account is active. To ask for your information to be deleted or corrected, email [your email].
